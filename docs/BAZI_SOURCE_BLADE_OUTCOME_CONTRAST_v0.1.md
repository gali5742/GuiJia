## Validation and boundaries

Canonical source, definition, relation and configuration review records must exactly match their finite registry. Runtime evaluation checks the complete current chart and actual profiles/evidence for all original endpoints or candidates, including scope, position, character, element and visible Ten God. Missing, duplicate, sparse or conflicting evidence prevents consumption. Precomputed target/execution injections do not bypass validation. Outputs are deeply immutable without mutating callers.
